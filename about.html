Operations should be simple enough for your team to own.

Why deknit

I started in customer support, and over the years I moved toward the work that decides whether a team can keep up as a business grows: the processes, tools, and automation behind the scenes.

One example. Our AI support agent needed live account data, and Engineering estimated at least a quarter to build the connection. I found the data already sat in a tool we paid for, built the connection myself, and we were live within a month. That's the pattern I keep following: use what a team already has before building something new, and keep checking that it works after launch.

I design the solution, build what I can with no-code tools and AI, and work with engineers on the rest. Then I kept hearing the same problems from owners of newer and smaller businesses, and I realized the approach fits them too. The same playbook that helps a growing fintech can help a growing business of any size move faster. So I started deknit.

What the name means

To deknit is to untangle. That's the whole idea.

What we believe

Good operations should feel clear and calm. Technology should serve the process, not the other way around. And the best work leaves your team more capable than before.
