Operations should be simple enough for your team to own.

Why deknit

A growing business shouldn't need you to hold every piece together.

But it often happens gradually. You add a tool to solve one problem, a manual step to work around another, and suddenly your team is spending hours keeping everything moving. The work gets done, but it takes more effort than it should.

I started deknit because I kept hearing that story from business owners, and I recognized it.

My career began in customer support and grew into leading customer success operations at a fintech with an international presence. Working close to customers, teams, and the systems behind them taught me how everyday friction builds up, and what it takes to untangle it.

What I learned is that better operations start with understanding how the work actually happens. Sometimes you need a new tool. Sometimes you already have what you need, but the pieces aren't connected. And any improvement needs to make sense to the people using it every day.

That's the thinking behind deknit.

We get to know your business, find what's making work harder, and help you put a better way of working in place. That might mean connecting your tools, automating repetitive tasks, or using AI to support your customers and team. We handle implementation, work with engineers when needed, and make sure your team knows how to use what we build.

Then we stay involved, checking what's working and adjusting as your business grows.

deknit exists to help you untangle your operations and keep them untangled, so running your business doesn't mean holding it all together yourself.

The name

deknit is a nod to untangling.

As a business grows, its tools, tasks, and processes become intertwined. Over time, it gets harder to see what's connected, what's necessary, and what's getting in the way.

Our work is to untangle that complexity and help the pieces work together more clearly. That idea gave deknit its name.

What we believe

Start with the business.

Understanding your people, priorities, and everyday challenges comes before recommending a tool.

Make better use of what you have.

Progress doesn't always require starting over. Sometimes the biggest improvement comes from connecting or simplifying what's already in place.

Make work easier for people.

AI and automation should reduce the burden on your team. A solution only works if people understand it, trust it, and can use it confidently.

Follow through.

Launching a solution is one step. We believe in checking the results, listening to the team, and improving as the business changes.
