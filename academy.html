Who it's for

Teams who know their operations need work and want the skills to fix and maintain them.

After the academy, your team will be able to:

  • Spot and name the tangles in their own operations

  • Know where automation and AI help, and where they don't
  • Build and maintain simple automations
  • Keep operations untangled as the business grows
