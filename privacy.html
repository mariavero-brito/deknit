Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026.

Who we are

deknit provides operations consulting, practical AI and automation support, and training. This notice explains how we handle information through this website, enquiries, and scheduling. The business responsible for this information is Maria V. Brito Noguera, doing business as deknit. For privacy questions, email legal@deknit.com.

Information you provide

We receive your name, email address, company, and the information you choose to share about your operations. Academy enquiries also request team size. When you book a call, Calendly collects booking details, your selected time, and any answers you provide. Please do not include passwords, payment card numbers, government identifiers, health information, or confidential customer records in enquiry forms or booking notes.

How forms and email work

When you submit our contact or academy waitlist form, your entries are sent directly to Formspree, which processes and stores submissions, filters spam, and sends notification emails directly to our business inbox. Formspree may also process technical information such as your IP address and browser information to operate the service and prevent abuse. Our free plan provides 30 days of submission history in Formspree; notification emails may remain in Gmail under our separate retention practices. See Formspree's Privacy Policy. Messages sent to hello@deknit.com, billing@deknit.com, and legal@deknit.com are forwarded through Porkbun to our Gmail inbox. Google processes email used to communicate with you.

Technical information and external services

Our website requests fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google when loading those fonts. The website code does not currently include advertising pixels or analytics scripts. This website is hosted by Netlify, which may process technical information such as your IP address and request logs to deliver and secure the site, under its own privacy notice. Booking links take you to Calendly, whose own privacy notice and cookie controls apply on its website. Calls use Google Meet. These providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser information, and service usage under their own notices.

How we use information

We use information to respond to your enquiries, arrange calls, understand your needs, provide requested services, maintain business records, and protect our systems. If you ask to join the academy waitlist, we use your contact information to send academy availability updates. You can ask us to stop those updates at any time. We do not treat an ordinary contact enquiry as consent to unrelated marketing.

Who receives information

Information is handled by the providers needed for the activities described above, including Formspree, Calendly, Google, and Porkbun. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights or prevent fraud. We do not sell enquiry information or use it for targeted advertising.

Client work and AI tools

This website notice does not authorize us to upload your business records to AI tools. Access to client systems, confidential information, and any AI processing will be covered by the applicable service agreement and, where needed, a data processing agreement. We will identify the agreed tools and purposes before processing client data through them.

Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including applicable legal, accounting, and dispute obligations. Form submissions stay in Formspree for the retention period of our plan, currently 30 days. Notification emails, Calendly booking records, and email correspondence are kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and keep business records, and we delete enquiry and waitlist records on request. Client records are kept for the length of the engagement and for any period required for legal, tax, and accounting purposes. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You can email legal@deknit.com to request access, correction, or deletion of information, or to stop academy updates. Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have additional rights, including objection, restriction, or portability. We may need to verify your identity and may retain records where legally required. If we process information on behalf of a client, we may direct your request to that client. You may also contact an applicable privacy regulator.

Location and intended audience

deknit operates from the United States. Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. We offer our services to clients in the United States only. This website is intended for adult business users, and we do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as our website or practices change. The effective date will appear at the top, and we will provide additional notice where required. Contact: legal@deknit.com.
