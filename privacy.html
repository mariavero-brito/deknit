Information you provide

We receive your name, email address, company, and the information you choose to share about your operations. Academy enquiries also request team size. When you book a call, Calendly collects booking details, your selected time, and any answers you provide. Please do not include passwords, payment card numbers, government identifiers, health information, or confidential customer records in enquiry forms or booking notes.

How forms and email work

When you submit our contact or academy waitlist form, your entries are sent directly to Formspree, which processes and stores submissions, filters spam, and sends notification emails directly to get.deknit@gmail.com. Formspree may also process technical information such as your IP address and browser information to operate the service and prevent abuse. Our free plan provides 30 days of submission history in Formspree; notification emails may remain in Gmail under our separate retention practices. See Formspree's Privacy Policy. Messages sent to hello@deknit.com are forwarded through Porkbun to our Gmail inbox at get.deknit@gmail.com. Google processes email used to communicate with you.

How we use information

We use information to respond to your enquiries, arrange calls, understand your needs, provide requested services, maintain business records, and protect our systems. We do not treat an ordinary contact enquiry as consent to unrelated marketing.
