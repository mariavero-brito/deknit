deknit Untangle

A diagnosis, your deknit map, and then fixes in priority order. For teams whose operations are straining as the business grows. See what's includedHide details

The diagnosis

We learn how your operations really work. We talk to your team, review your tools and processes, and name every tangle.

We look across ten areas:

  1. Customers and clients
  2. Leads and sales
  3. Contracts and paperwork
  4. Capacity and scheduling
  5. Money and payments
  6. Marketing operations
  7. Team and hiring
  8. Customer service
  9. Tools and data
  10. Owner and growth

You receive the deknit map

It's a simple, easy-to-read report with:

  • Each tangle we found, named clearly
  • How much each one matters (impact and urgency)
  • A tool map showing which of your tools overlap, which could be cancelled, and which could be replaced by a single solution, with estimated savings
  • A suggested order to fix things
  • What your team can handle and where we lead
  • Our estimate of how long the untangling will take

The deknit map is yours to keep, even if you don't continue.

The fixes

We work through the map in priority order. We fix process, connect your tools, build automations, and use AI where it genuinely helps. Engagements run from one month to a year or more, depending on how deep the tangle goes.

You can stop at any time with 2 weeks notice.
