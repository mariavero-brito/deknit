Month 1

We learn how your operations really work. We talk to your team, review your tools and processes, and name every tangle. We look across ten areas:

  1. Customers and clients
  2. Leads and sales
  3. Contracts and paperwork
  4. Capacity and scheduling
  5. Money and payments
  6. Marketing operations
  7. Team and hiring
  8. Customer service
  9. Tools and data
  10. Owner and growth

You receive the deknit map

It is a plain-language report with:

  • each tangle we found, named clearly
  • how much each one matters (impact and urgency)
  • a tool map showing which of your tools overlap, which could be cancelled, and which could be replaced by a single solution, with estimated savings
  • a suggested order to fix things
  • what your team can handle and where we lead
  • our estimate of how many months the untangling will take

The deknit map is yours to keep, even if you don't continue.

Month 2 and beyond

We work through the map in priority order. We fix process, connect your tools, build automations, and use AI where it genuinely helps. Engagements run from one month to a year or more, depending on how deep the tangle goes. You can stop at any time with 2 weeks notice.

Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Everything is month to month. Cancel yourself in your customer portal at least 2 weeks before your next billing date and you won't be billed for the following month.

How does billing work?

Billed monthly in advance by card through Stripe. In your customer portal you can update your payment method, see invoices, and cancel.

What happens to the deknit map if I leave?

It's yours to keep, along with everything we built and documented.

How long does untangling take?

It depends on how deep the tangle goes. Month 1 gives you a clear estimate, and it can range from one month to a year or more.

Will you tell me which tools to cancel?

Yes. The tool map in your deknit map shows overlaps and options. We don't take commissions from any tool, so our advice stays independent, and you make the final call. Savings are estimates, based on current plans.

Do you run our marketing?

We automate the busywork behind your marketing, like tracking where new clients come from, follow-up emails, review requests, and keeping your listings consistent. We don't run your marketing for you.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We handle the technical work and explain everything in plain language.

Will AI replace my team?

No. We use AI to remove repetitive work so your team can focus on work that needs judgment and care.

What if I only want training?

That's deknit Academy. Self-paced lessons start with a short intake so you know where to begin.
