Cancelling and billing

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Everything is month to month. Cancel yourself in your customer portal at least 2 weeks before your next billing date and you won't be billed for the following month.

How does billing work?

Billed monthly in advance by card through Stripe. In your customer portal you can update your payment method, see invoices, and cancel.

What happens to the deknit map if I leave?

It's yours to keep, along with everything we built and documented.

How we work

How long does untangling take?

It depends on how deep the tangle goes. The deknit map gives you our estimate up front, and it can range from a short engagement to a year or more.

Will you tell me which tools to cancel?

Yes. The tool map in your deknit map shows overlaps and options. We don't take commissions from any tool, so our advice stays independent, and you make the final call. Savings are estimates, based on current plans.

Do you run our marketing?

We automate the busywork behind your marketing, like tracking where new clients come from, follow-up emails, review requests, and keeping your listings consistent. We don't run your marketing for you.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We handle the technical work and explain everything clearly.

Will AI replace my team?

No. We use AI to remove repetitive work so your team can focus on work that needs judgment and care.

Academy

What if I only want training?

That's deknit Academy. It's self-paced lessons you can start on your own, with optional live chat time if you want to talk things through with me.
